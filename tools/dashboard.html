👋 Selamat datang!

Aplikasi ini mencari lowongan magang/kerja dari LinkedIn, JobStreet, dan Glints, lalu membantu kamu melacak lamaran. Mulai dalam 3 langkah:

  1. Isi kata kunci di tab scraper, mis. Electrical Engineer Intern (ketik lalu Enter).
  2. Tekan ▶ Jalankan. Hasil muncul sebagai kartu, lengkap dengan kecocokan, syarat, dan deadline.
  3. Tekan + Track pada lowongan yang menarik untuk menyimpannya di Lamaran saya.

🔔 Di aplikasi ini pencarian bisa berjalan otomatis saat aplikasi dibuka dan kamu dapat notifikasi lowongan baru. Atur di ⚙️ Setelan.

Connect LinkedIn session (optional)

LinkedIn's Jobs section only covers formal job postings. A lot of magang openings in Indonesia get shared as regular feed posts instead - this lets the scraper search those too, using your own already-logged-in session.

⚠️ This uses your personal LinkedIn session cookie, which is equivalent to being logged in as you. Automated searching like this is against LinkedIn's Terms of Service and carries real risk of your account getting rate-limited or restricted - there's no way to fully guarantee against it. Only proceed if you accept that risk, and avoid running it too frequently.
  1. Log into linkedin.com in your own regular browser (skip if already logged in)
  2. Press F12 to open DevTools → Application tab (Chrome) or Storage (Edge/Firefox) → Cookies → https://www.linkedin.com
  3. Find the row named li_at, click it, and copy its Value
  4. Paste it below - it's saved straight to your local .env file, never sent anywhere else from this page
